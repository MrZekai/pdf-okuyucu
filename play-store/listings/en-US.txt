TITLE
PDF Reader: Fast & Private

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Open PDFs fast, read in night mode, and keep documents safely on your device.

FULL DESCRIPTION
PDF Reader is a local-first app designed to make viewing documents fast, simple, and secure.

Choose PDF files from your device or download them directly from a web link. Documents stay in the app’s private on-device folder; you do not need an account or upload files to a server operated by the developer to read them.

Key features:

• Pick and open PDFs from your device
• Download PDFs from a URL
• Vertical and horizontal reading
• Single-page snap mode
• Pinch zoom and double-tap zoom
• Night mode for PDF pages
• Favourites and a searchable local library
• Last-page and reading-progress metadata
• Password-protected PDF support
• Document sharing
• Turkish, English, and Spanish interface

PDF Reader keeps your documents organised, stores reading information on your device, and gives you quick access to the tools you need through a clean interface.

The app contains ads. Ad privacy choices can be managed from Settings.
